How it works

Overview

GhostCall routes every call through the Tor onion network, encrypted end-to-end with the Noise_XX protocol. Your identity is a Starknet wallet — pseudonymous by default, anonymous if you choose.

Handles are registered on-chain. Call routing happens over Nostr NIP-59 gift-wrapped events. Payments clear via ERC-5564 stealth addresses and an optional STRK20 privacy pool.

Nothing touches a central server. Built for a world where privacy isn't a feature — it's the whole point.
